# Terms and Policy
Version 2026-11-15
Draft: to be checked by the gym owner, or someone qualified, before it is relied on.

These terms cover your gym membership and how this app uses your information. By creating an account you agree to them.

## Your account
You sign in with your email and password. Keep your password to yourself; anything done with your account is treated as done by you. Your name must be your real first and last name, because the front desk checks it when you come in.

## Packages and terms
A package gives you gym access for a set number of days, called a term. A term you buy while another is running starts when your last paid term ends. A term bought after your last one has ended starts as soon as the payment is verified. There are no upgrades, no automatic charges and no credit for unused time when you buy another package.

## Paying
You can pay by QR transfer and send the reference and a screenshot in the app, or pay cash or GCash at the front desk. A QR payment must be paid and sent within the time shown in the app, or it expires and the package isn't bought. A payment counts once the front desk has checked it. Discount codes work once per person, only for what each code says.

## Refunds
Refunds: only in a term's first 7 days, for the days not used. You ask in the app, under My membership. The amount is the days left on the day staff approve your request, so the sooner it is approved, the more you get back. A term bought ahead can be cancelled for a full refund any time before it starts. If you have checked in during the term, staff review the request and may decline it. The app sends no money: staff pay you back in cash or by GCash and record it.

## Coming in
Check in at the front desk with the QR code in the app, your desk PIN, your account password, or a valid ID. Your membership must be active. Each check-in is recorded with the time and how you checked in.

## Amenity bookings
You can book an amenity at least 1 hour ahead and up to 14 days ahead, alone or with a coach. A booking is held by a down payment, set by the gym; the rest is paid at the desk. Cancel 24 hours or more before it starts to get the down payment back; later than that, it isn't refunded. If the gym cancels, or closes that day, you get it back.

## Coaches and messages
You can hire a coach, book sessions with them and message them in the app. Messages with coaches and with the front desk are kept, and staff can read messages sent to the front desk.

## Gym closing days
The gym may close on some days. Closing days are announced in the app, and bookings on those days are cancelled and refunded.

## Conduct
Follow staff instructions and the gym's house rules, and treat other members and staff with respect. The gym may archive an account that is used to break these terms.

## Your information
The app keeps your name, email, phone number and photo if you add them, your memberships, payments and refunds, check-ins, bookings, and messages. It also keeps a record of important actions, with the network address they came from, which is cleared after 90 days. The gym uses this only to run your membership, take payments, check you in and keep its accounts.

Staff see what they need for their work. Other members can't see your details; a coach you hire sees your name and photo. Your information is not sold.

You can ask the front desk to correct your information, or to close your account. Payment and refund records are kept for the gym's accounts after an account is closed.

## Changes
If these terms change, the app shows the new version and its date.
