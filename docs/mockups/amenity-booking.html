Theme Member, Windows

1. Member: Book an amenity (Windows)

The page opens on the amenity cards: status, price per session, the coaches who run it (F14) and Notify me, as on today's Amenities half, plus Book. "Coaches & amenities" goes back to plain Coaches (D30 reversed); the old half-hour grid goes.

Book opens a sheet. Left: the month in the coach Dashboard's style (today on yellow, the chosen day outlined, days outside the next 14 dimmed, a dot on days you already have a booking). Right: the start times still free that day, how long (30 minutes to 2 hours), how you'll pay, and the total. Times that are taken, in a cooldown, or under 1 hour away are struck through.

Book and pay holds the time. Paying by GCash or QR opens the same "send proof" step as Packages; "Pay at the desk" just shows the code to give the cashier. The desk approves it in Payments to verify, and you get "Booking confirmed".

One thing to confirm: because an unpaid hold ends 1 hour before the start (F12), a time less than 1 hour away can't be booked online. A member with a pass or an unused session still books right up to the start, as today.

3. Cancel and "I didn't use it"

Cancel on a paid booking 1 hour or more before: one confirm, and the refund is approved at once ("₱150 will be repaid at the front desk"). Within the hour the button is gone and the row says why. Cancelling an unpaid hold just releases the time.

I didn't use it appears from the end of the booking for 24 hours and asks for a short reason.

5. Front desk: Payments to verify (Windows only)

Amenity bookings join the list, with the booked time and how they'll pay. Approving a "Pay at the desk" one takes the cash into your drawer (it needs an open shift, as at the Cashier).

Below it, Booking refund claims: what the member wrote, next to what the desk needs to check (check-ins that day and the amenity's status), with Approve and Decline, each with a reason.

One thing to confirm: F13 said claims use Refunds & voids, but that page is for Admin and System admin only. So the front desk decides claims here; approved refunds (from claims and from cancels) then show in Refunds & voids, where they're repaid and recorded as membership refunds are.
