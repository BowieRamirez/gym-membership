1. Member: Calendar (Windows)

A new menu item, right under My membership. The month is the coach Dashboard's: today on yellow, the chosen day outlined, each day naming its first two entries in a word (Session, Open slot, Coach shift, or the amenity) and "+2 more"; the times and full names are in the day's list. A dot says what each entry is: your session (yellow, ink in light), amenity booking (green), your coach's open time (blue ring) and your coach on the floor (grey).

The chosen day is listed on the right. An open time has Ask for this time: it sends a time request with the same rules as Time requests (one request at a time, none while you already have a session booked). A booking has Open, which goes to Book an amenity. Sessions and shifts are just shown. The member doesn't book gym visits here: there's no capacity to book.

3. The member menu follows the membership (F16)

With an active membership the everyday pages lead and Packages moves near the end as Renew or change. Without one, Packages comes first so buying is the obvious next step; members-only pages stay listed and open their "get a membership" notice, as today. The phone keeps the first three as tabs plus Messages; the rest are under More.

4. Coach: Profile & settings (F17, Windows and phone)

One menu item instead of "My coach profile" and "Settings". The coach profile comes first, exactly as it is today (at the gym, photo, specialty, rate, about you, taking new members, Save profile); the account settings follow under their own heading, also unchanged (name, sign-in email, theme, password). The account menu's Settings opens this page too.
