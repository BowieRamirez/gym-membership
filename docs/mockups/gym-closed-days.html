Phase 7: gym closed days (G16, G17, G18 as you decided)

Admin and System admin close a whole day on Gym settings, with a reason. Closing happens at once (it isn't part of "Save settings"), after a confirm that says what will be cancelled and the exact announcement everyone gets.

  • Upcoming closed days have Open again. Past ones stay in the list as history, marked Past.
  • Members see a closed day crossed out in red on the booking sheet and as "Closed" on their calendar, with the reason. Coaches see no shift form that day, just the reason.
  • Front desk: check-in that day is refused with "The gym is closed that day. See Announcements for why…". No new screen for that.
