1. Cashier: your drawer (front desk, Admin, System admin; Windows only)

The Cashier page starts with your drawer. With no shift open, it asks for the float you counted (the Gym settings amount is filled in; a different amount needs a note). Sales stay usable for GCash and bank transfers, but Cash says why it can't complete.

While the shift is open the same strip shows since when, the float, cash taken, cash paid back and the expected cash. It replaces "Net cash today", which added up every cashier's cash and so never matched one drawer. End shift opens the count in place: type what's in the drawer, the difference shows as you type, and a note is asked only when it differs.
