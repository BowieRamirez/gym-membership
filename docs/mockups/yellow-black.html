Rules

  • Yellow is the signal: the side menu (light), the selected item, the one highlighted figure, attention bars, the main action and "needs action" tags. Not page backgrounds or every card.
  • In light, yellow is only ever a filled surface with black on it; the focus ring and thin lines are black. In dark, the focus ring is yellow.
  • Text on yellow is always black. Text on black is white or yellow.
  • Green and red only for status words, always beside the word. Pending and late use a yellow dot (outlined in light).
  • Fonts unchanged: Barlow, Barlow Condensed for figures.
