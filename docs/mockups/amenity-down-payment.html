1. Member: Book an amenity (Windows)

New in the sheet: Solo or With a coach. With a coach lists only coaches who run this amenity, have a shift over the whole time and aren't booked then; the amenity's coach fee is added. A code box shows the codes that fit this amenity. The price is split: down payment now (50%, set in Gym settings) and the balance at the desk. The slot is only yours once the desk verifies the down payment.

3. Front desk: Payments to verify and Amenity status (Windows only)

A booking's row says it's a down payment, the coach, and the balance still due. Verifying it confirms the booking; if another down payment for that slot or coach was verified first, the booking is cancelled "Slot taken" and the money goes straight to Refunds. On Amenity status, today's confirmed bookings with a balance show Take balance (cash into your drawer, or GCash with a reference).
