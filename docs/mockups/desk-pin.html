1. Front desk Check-in: the PIN step (Windows only)

Check in on a member found by name no longer checks them in straight away. The row opens a PIN step in place, with the member's photo (or initials) so staff can compare the face. The member types their 4-digit desk PIN on the desk keyboard; it shows as dots. Nothing is recorded until the PIN is right. Admin and System admin also see a reason field for a member with no PIN. While the step is open, its Check in is yellow; the step closes on success or Cancel.

2. Member QR: one use, five minutes (Windows side card and phone)

The QR on My membership is now a pass that works once and lasts 5 minutes. It renews itself 15 seconds before it runs out, so the member never has to press anything. The line under it says when it changes, in words. A screenshot or the old permanent code scans as "Old code".

4. Admin and System admin: who checked in by name (Windows only)

The Overview's check-ins for a day gain two columns: who recorded each one, and the reason when an Admin skipped the PIN. A "By name only" switch narrows the list to manual check-ins, which is the "Manual check-ins" list from F8 without a new page.
