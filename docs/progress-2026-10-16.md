## 2. What was done most recently (phases 4a and 4b)

- **Messenger (4a).** One chat for all roles. Members get a Coach tab and a Staff tab (a guest with no plan can still write to the front desk). Front desk gets an inbox they can take, release, close and reopen. Search finds people and message text. Messages load 50 at a time, so the old "chat does not load reliably" problem (it downloaded everything and silently cut off) is gone. New messages appear by looking again every 6 seconds.
- **Photos in chat (4b).** One photo per message with an optional caption, between member and coach and between member and front desk. A photo-only message reads "Photo" in the list. Staff and admins cannot see coach chat photos; guests and ended hires can still read old ones but not send.
- **Review fixes.** An independent review of each phase found real problems and they were fixed with tests: front desk chats could not load on a real server; a half-typed message could follow you into another conversation; a staff member who was deactivated could keep a conversation locked; a chat full of photos asked for too many links at once.
- **Clearer error.** When the server lacks something the app asks for, the app now says "This part of the app needs a server update that hasn't been installed yet. Tell the gym owner." instead of "Something went wrong."
- **Checked against your real project.** Migrations `0013` to `0016` are **not there yet**, which is why Messages shows the error right now. A test account (not recorded here) can sign in, so the project itself is reachable.

**Checks at the end of this work:** 296 unit tests and 103 database checks pass; the Android and Windows apps build. All of it was also tried by hand in demo mode on Windows and on the Android phone layout.

## 6. Small things deferred on purpose (not blocking)

Not fixed yet, noted so they are not lost:
- Chat: more than 50 new messages arriving between two refreshes would leave a gap; the Staff/Coach tab is detected by the name "Front desk"; staff can see their own user ids in a thread (never names).
- Photos: a failed send after the upload leaves an unseen file in storage (no delete rule yet); a mislabelled non-image file is only checked by its ending and size; an app left open for over an hour shows expired links on older photos; there is no tap-to-enlarge.
- Phase 4c: a change signal that lands while the page is busy sending is dropped (the next look can be 30 seconds away); the refresh pace is adopted on the next tick; last active can lag up to about 10 minutes; the typing throttle is shared across conversations; the typing and online channels are public (ids only, no names); not yet confirmed that Realtime rejoins its channels by itself after a dropped connection (check with airplane mode on a device); the header keeps marking you "active" while the app is minimised.
- Phase 5, for the owner to decide: a retry after a lost network response can record a second walk-in day pass (membership, amenity and renewal payments have server duplicate guards, walk-ins do not; fixing it needs a request id in the database function); the member picked at the Cashier stays selected after a payment (as the old Counter did), so check the name before the next one.
- Phase 5, small: the page timer can keep polling from a hidden page if you leave during the first load (affects every self-refreshing page, fix is a visible flag in `BasePage`); tiles and item cards may not be announced as buttons by a screen reader; on a 360 dp phone the tiles and cards show one per row instead of two (use about 148 wide); "Unread messages" also counts chats other staff have taken; check-ins "today" uses the device's midnight while the cash summary uses Manila time; changing the member for a membership or amenity drops the picked card; the disabled "Complete payment" button gives no reason; Home downloads whole row sets every 20 seconds (fine for one gym).
- From phases 1 to 3: avatar tap target size, keyboard focus in rows, stopping timers when a page closes, a past return time pre-filled in the amenity form, Asia/Manila time on alerts.
