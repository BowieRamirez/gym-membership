### Phase 7 plan: agreed scope

**Extensions only; no upgrades or downgrades**
- Remove the immediate-upgrade flow and unused-time credit calculations from Phase 7.
- Members choose a Monthly (30-day), Quarterly (90-day) or Annual (365-day) extension, even if it differs from their current package. Charge the selected package's price less any eligible discount, with the final amount shown before confirmation.
- Keep the current paid term unchanged. Only a verified payment creates paid entitlement to the next term; an unverified payment must not grant access.
- Schedule the extension after the last existing paid term, with no overlaps or lost prepaid days. If no paid term remains, start the new term when payment is verified.
- Show the current term and each paid future term separately, with package, dates, status and payment details. Keep historical package, duration and amount-paid information rather than deriving old transactions from today's catalog prices.

**One member action: Cancel & request refund**
- Use one combined button per eligible paid term, not separate cancellation and refund buttons. Confirmation identifies the selected term and the exact amount requested.
- Allow submission only within 24 hours of the selected term's payment, not until midnight and not from the future term's activation date. After the deadline, block new member requests on both the screen and the server.
- Refund 100% of the amount actually paid after discounts, never the original list price or an unused-time credit.
- A timely request may be reviewed and repaid after the 24-hour window. Submission does not automatically cancel membership or transfer money.

**Admin approval controls cancellation**
- Pending: the selected term, current access and scheduled dates remain unchanged.
- Rejected: leave the selected term unchanged and show the admin's decision/reason.
- Approved: cancel the selected term and record its refund as awaiting manual repayment. If it is the current term, that term no longer grants access; if it is a future extension, the current term remains unchanged. Access from any other valid term is assessed separately.
- Repaid: after returning the money manually, the admin records repayment and marks the refund paid. Approval and actual repayment must be distinguishable; the app does not send bank/e-wallet transfers.
- Only admins may approve/reject requests or record repayment. Keep decisions, amounts, actors and timestamps for audit.

**Implemented locally**
- Migration `0018_membership_terms_and_refunds.sql` adds `membership_terms`, `refund_requests` and server-set payment snapshots. Existing membership/renewal records remain compatibility references; exact uncancelled term windows determine access.
- Backfill preserves recorded original/renewal windows and actual payment amounts. Old catalog names/durations cannot be recovered, so those snapshots are labelled `legacy_catalog`. Ambiguous or overlapping paid history aborts the migration instead of silently changing dates. Legacy access with no verified payment cannot be refunded.
- Protected server operations handle extensions, strict 24-hour requests, admin decisions and separate manual repayments. Member/payment locks prevent concurrent duplicate scheduling, approval, repayment and simultaneous void/refund. Direct ledger writes are denied; members read their own, admins manage refunds.
- My membership lists paid terms and their request/decision/repayment state. Packages creates a selected-package extension; retry retains that selected package. Admin has a Refunds queue with confirmation and separate repayment entry. Packages remain text-only.
- Access checks use canonical windows, including cancellation gaps. DemoGateway mirrors the rules. Revenue and cash summaries subtract actual repayments on the repayment day/method; approval alone does not reduce receipts. No money-transfer integration was added.

**Final details approved by the owner**
- The 24-hour request window starts at staff verification/recording (`verified_at`), not the QR transfer timestamp. Requests must arrive strictly before `verified_at + 24 hours`; exactly 24 hours is closed. This supersedes the earlier same-calendar-day wording.
- Later queued terms keep their original dates when an earlier term is cancelled. Show any resulting gap; do not silently move or delete another paid term.
- Local implementation is approved. The technical plan and staged checklist are in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-17-role-redesign-phase7.md`. Live database changes and commit/push are not yet approved.
